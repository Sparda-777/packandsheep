Services

From your stock to your customers.

A service focused on order fulfilment and shipping, with storage and shipment tracking integrated into the workflow.

Storage

Your stock can be stored partially or in full in my secure, heated and ventilated premises.

Order fulfilment

Your orders are prepared quickly and carefully using protective packaging suited to your products.

Shipping

I handle postage, labelling and dispatch of your parcels.

Shipment tracking

I provide live shipment tracking and your customers receive email updates as their order makes its way to them.

How does it work?

A simple process.

1

Your stock arrives

Products are organised to make order fulfilment simple and reliable.

2

You send the orders

All the information needed for fulfilment and shipping is centralised.

3

I pick and pack

Each order is assembled and protected according to its contents.

4

The parcel is shipped

The label is created, the parcel is dispatched and the shipment is tracked.

5

You are invoiced

Each month, you are invoiced for every parcel prepared, according to its type.
