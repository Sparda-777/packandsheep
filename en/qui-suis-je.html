Behind Pack & Sheep

About me

Active in the music scene since 2006, I started out as a musician in various bands playing metal music. I created my own label with two friends in 2009 and volunteered there for more than 15 years.

In 2026 I decided to leave my job, start my own business and offer my shipping services to other cultural businesses. It also gives me the flexibility to organise my time and develop my artistic work.

The name

Why “Pack & Sheep”?

Because at Pack & Sheep, I pack parcels and ship them! But “Pack & Ship” felt a little too impersonal, and since I love animals, I called it Pack & Sheep 😂 So I took a black Ouessant ram, stuck a shipping label on him and sent him on his way.

The most curious among you may spot another pun in the name, as well as an Easter egg hidden in the logo. Happy hunting!
